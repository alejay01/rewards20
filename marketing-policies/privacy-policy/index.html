Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Boudin Company collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with Boudin Rewards, our website, and our marketing communications.

Effective date: July 22, 2026

1. Information we collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, including your name, email address, mobile telephone number, birthday, favorite products, account credentials or identifiers, loyalty activity, rewards, transaction-related information, customer-service communications, and marketing preferences.

When you enroll in text messaging, we also maintain records of your consent, including the date, time, source, and language presented when you opted in, as well as opt-out and help requests.

Our website and service providers may automatically collect limited technical information, such as browser type, device type, IP address, pages viewed, and approximate location derived from an IP address. We use this information to operate, secure, and improve our services.

2. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • create, maintain, and authenticate Boudin Rewards accounts;
  • calculate points, provide rewards, and maintain loyalty history;
  • process requests and provide customer support;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  • maintain consent, opt-out, and compliance records;
  • improve our restaurants, website, loyalty program, and communications; and
  • send rewards and promotional text messages only when you have separately and affirmatively opted in.

3. Mobile information and SMS consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except with service providers acting on our behalf to deliver and support the messaging program. Those providers may use the information only to perform services for us and may not use it for their own marketing.

We do not use purchased, rented, or third-party marketing lists to enroll people in the Boudin Rewards SMS program. SMS consent is specific to The Boudin Company and is not transferred to another business.

4. Boudin Rewards text messages

If you opt in, you may receive recurring automated text messages from The Boudin Company about loyalty points, earned or available rewards, birthday rewards, customer-care information, restaurant promotions, limited-time offers, and win-back offers. You may receive up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.

SMS enrollment is optional and is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. For complete program rules, review our SMS Terms & Conditions.

5. When we disclose information

We may disclose information to service providers that support website hosting, databases, security, analytics, loyalty operations, messaging delivery, and customer service. These providers are authorized to use information only as necessary to provide services to us.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or a valid government request; to protect rights, safety, and security; to investigate fraud or abuse; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets, subject to applicable law. The mobile-information restrictions described above continue to apply.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for site functionality, security, remembering preferences, and understanding site usage. You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling necessary cookies may affect site features.

7. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the program, provide services, document consent and opt-outs, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information. No security method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You may unsubscribe from marketing texts at any time by replying STOP. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Certain transaction, security, consent, suppression, or legal records may be retained when required or permitted by law.

9. Children

Boudin Rewards and our marketing program are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through these services. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the effective date. When required, we will provide additional notice or obtain new consent.
