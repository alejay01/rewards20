These terms govern The Boudin Company Boudin Rewards recurring text messaging program.

Effective date: July 22, 2026

1. Program name and description

The program is called The Boudin Company Boudin Rewards SMS Program. When you affirmatively opt in, The Boudin Company may send recurring automated text messages about loyalty points, earned or available rewards, birthday rewards, customer-care information, restaurant promotions, limited-time offers, and win-back offers.

2. Opt-in and consent

You may enroll by providing a valid mobile number through The Boudin Company’s Boudin Rewards enrollment form and checking the separate, unchecked box that expressly agrees to receive recurring rewards and marketing text messages from The Boudin Company. Your consent applies only to this program and is not transferred or assigned.

By opting in, you confirm that you are the subscriber or customary user of the mobile number provided and that you are authorized to enroll it. Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

3. Message frequency and charges

You may receive up to 4 recurring messages per month. Message frequency may vary based on loyalty activity and available offers. Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless plan. Please contact your wireless carrier with questions about your plan.

4. How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any program message to unsubscribe. You may receive one final message confirming your opt-out. After that confirmation, no further marketing messages will be sent through this program unless you provide new consent.

We may also recognize customary opt-out keywords such as STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE, and OPTOUT.

5. Help and customer care

Reply HELP to any program message for assistance. You may also call 713-561-5645 or email info@theboudincompany.com.

6. Delivery and participating carriers

Text message delivery is subject to effective transmission by your wireless carrier and is not guaranteed. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Program availability may vary by carrier and device.

7. Privacy

Our Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, retain, disclose, and protect information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be sold or rented.
