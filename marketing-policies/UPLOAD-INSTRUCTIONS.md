# The Boudin Company marketing-policy upload package

Upload the entire `marketing-policies` folder to the public web root for `theboudincompany.com`. Keep the folder names and `policy.css` in the same structure.

After upload, the public pages should be:

- Policy hub: `https://theboudincompany.com/marketing-policies/`
- Privacy Policy: `https://theboudincompany.com/marketing-policies/privacy-policy/`
- SMS Terms & Conditions: `https://theboudincompany.com/marketing-policies/sms-terms-and-conditions/`

## Twilio campaign fields

Use these exact URLs in the Twilio A2P 10DLC Campaign form:

- **Privacy Policy URL:** `https://theboudincompany.com/marketing-policies/privacy-policy/`
- **Terms and Conditions URL:** `https://theboudincompany.com/marketing-policies/sms-terms-and-conditions/`

Do not submit the Twilio campaign until both URLs load publicly without a login and without redirecting to another domain.

## Important pre-publish checks

1. The verified company domain is spelled `theboudincompany.com`. The requested spelling `theboudancompany.com` did not resolve when this package was prepared.
2. The policies promise no more than 4 recurring messages per month. If the program will send more, update that number consistently in both HTML pages and the enrollment consent language before publishing.
3. Confirm the company name, address, telephone number, and email are still correct.
4. Keep the SMS consent checkbox separate from general account terms and unchecked by default.
5. Retain proof of each customer’s consent, including the disclosure shown, date, time, source page, and telephone number.

These pages were prepared to address current Twilio A2P 10DLC review criteria, but they are not a substitute for advice from a licensed attorney.
